PF

Built on the belief that every brand has a story worth telling.

Picket Fence Media started in 2019 in a garage in Louisville, Kentucky — two friends, a shared laptop, and a stubborn belief that marketing doesn't have to feel like marketing.

Today we're a team of 28 creatives, strategists, and data nerds spread across the Midwest, still obsessed with the same thing: making brands feel less like corporations and more like neighbors you'd actually want to borrow a cup of sugar from.

  • Authenticity over algorithms
  • Community-first thinking
  • Data-informed, never data-obsessed
  • No jargon, ever
